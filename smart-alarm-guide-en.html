Zhijie: Shift Alarm Support

Set workday alarms, check tomorrow's schedule and find help with Pro purchases.

Contact support

Email maque0425@163.com for help with the app or to suggest a change. Include the app version, your iOS version and the steps that led to the issue. Share only the information needed to describe the problem.

Choose a workday calendar

The interface is available in Simplified and Traditional Chinese. On first launch you can choose a calendar. To change it later, open Settings, then 假日地区与日历 (假日地區與日曆 in Traditional Chinese) and select 切换地区 (切換地區).

For Singapore, choose 新加坡. The workday rule runs Monday to Friday and skips public holidays and observed days published by Singapore's Ministry of Manpower. Use custom weekdays, a roster or a one-date exception for employer-arranged days off. Each workday alarm retains its own calendar when you change the default. Check the alarm's schedule or use the calendar button on the Home screen to see upcoming dates.

Check an alarm

Zhijie requires iOS 26 or later and alarm permission. If an alarm is missing, open it in the app and check its repeat rule, calendar and scheduled dates. Open the app when prompted to review or extend upcoming date-based alarms.

Restore or manage Pro

Open Pro in Settings and tap 恢复购买 (回復購買 in Traditional Chinese) with the Apple Account used for the original purchase. Manage or cancel monthly and annual subscriptions under Subscriptions in your Apple Account settings. The three-day free experience is claimed separately and ends without automatic billing.
