Zhijie: Shift Alarm Privacy Policy

Updated and effective 17 November 2026

The developer of Zhijie: Shift Alarm provides the app. This policy explains how the app handles alarms, timers, Pro features and related data. See the contact details below for questions about privacy.

1. Data stored on the device

You can use the app without a developer account. The app stores alarm times, labels, repeat rules, groups, sounds, snooze settings, timer state and ring and stop events received by the device. It also stores leave allowances, expiry dates, leave history, reminder links, manual sleep entries and any sleep records you choose to import from Apple Health. The app uses this data for reminders and reports and does not upload it to the developer's servers.

When you export a backup, the app packages alarms, groups, custom alarm audio and leave dates in an unencrypted file and saves it to a location you choose with the system file picker. If you choose iCloud Drive or another file service, that service stores and transfers the file. Restore reads only the file you select and lets you preview and confirm what to merge. Backups may include labels or recordings. You can delete a backup from the location where it is stored.

When you trim imported audio, the app reads the file you select and creates a temporary local copy to calculate the waveform, trim the sound and apply fades. When you save, the selected segment is kept as a WAV file in the sound library or for the alarm being edited. Cancelling, closing or finishing the editor clears its temporary copy. Your original file remains under your control. Audio is processed without uploading it to a server.

The app uses AlarmKit to schedule alarms. iOS controls how alarm labels and prompts appear on the Lock Screen, system ringing screen and Dynamic Island. History and trends use the events received on the device and may be incomplete. They do not establish when you woke up or slept.

Sounds imported from Files and voice recordings you save are stored in the app's local directory. The app accesses only the files you select. It asks for microphone permission before recording, clears unsaved recordings when you leave the recorder and limits each recording to 29 seconds.

2. Sleep records and Apple Health

You can enter sleep and wake times manually. The app labels the resulting duration as an estimate. If you choose to import sleep data, the app requests permission to read Apple Health sleep records. It does not write to Apple Health, send health data to the developer, weather or holiday services, or use health data for advertising or tracking.

Apple Health may return no records. The app does not infer your permission status or whether you slept from an empty response. You can still enter data manually. Imported times and their sources are stored locally for reports. You can delete those local copies in the app and manage future access in Apple Health settings. Deleting local copies does not delete the original Apple Health records. Manual entries take priority, and overlapping times and sources are not counted twice.

3. Voice input

Apple processes speech for Siri and Shortcuts. The app receives the date, duration and optional label needed to carry out the request.

For in-app voice entry, the app requests microphone and speech recognition permissions. It uses Chinese speech recognition on the device when supported and does not fall back to server recognition or retain the original audio long term. The result is an editable draft that you confirm before creating the alarm. You can type if recognition is unavailable or permission is denied.

4. Network requests for public information

The app fetches public calendars for the current and following year based on your selected calendar and the regions assigned to saved alarms. Sources are chinese-days through jsDelivr for mainland China, the Ministry of Manpower via data.gov.sg for Singapore, the Hong Kong Government's 1823 service, Taiwan's Directorate-General of Personnel Administration and the Macao SAR Government Portal. Calendar data is cached on the device. The app does not calculate individual or employer-arranged substitute holidays. Calendar requests use public data, region and year, and do not include alarm labels, recordings, sleep or leave records. The app's holiday settings list the sources, licences and calendar scope.

If you enable the morning briefing and explicitly stop a selected alarm scheduled between 07:30 and 12:00 (UTC+8), the app can read out the Gregorian date, day of the week, lunar date and any applicable solar term in the device's current time zone. Solar terms are calculated for 1900–2100. You can select a weather city or grant one-time location access to save a weather location. The app does not request background location or continuously track you. You can update your location manually when travelling. If you set a location and request weather, Apple WeatherKit receives the selected coordinates to return current weather and the day's forecast. Without a location or an available forecast, the briefing reads the date only. After stopping an alarm from the system screen, playback needs the app to come to the foreground. You can also play the briefing from the app. The app no longer fetches, displays or reads third-party news, and clears the old news cache when the briefing service first starts after an upgrade.

Calendar requests connect directly to jsDelivr, data.gov.sg, Hong Kong's 1823 service, Taiwan's Directorate-General of Personnel Administration or the Macao Government Portal at gov.mo. Weather and place-name requests connect to Apple weather and geocoding services. The services and their network providers receive the IP address, request time and URL, and technical information needed to complete the connection. Their policies govern how they handle and retain that information. Weather queries also contain the selected coordinates. Requests do not deliberately attach alarm labels, recordings, photos, sleep records or leave allowances. The developer does not receive those records or the weather location.

See the jsDelivr Privacy Policy and the Apple Privacy Policy. The weather interface shows Apple Weather attribution and a link to source information.

The app does not include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs and does not use request information for advertising or tracking. An offline device or unavailable service can still stop alarms. External links open only when selected and are subject to the destination site's policies.

5. In-app purchases, device backups and sharing

Apple App Store and StoreKit handle Pro purchases. The app gets product information, starts purchases and grants access based on transactions verified by Apple. The app does not collect card details or payment passwords. It calls Apple's restore flow when you ask to restore purchases. You can restore eligible purchases with the same Apple Account. The app does not automatically sync alarms or reports across devices.

iOS device backups may include local app data. Your Apple Account and device settings control those backups. The app does not use developer account services, sell personal data or use health, sleep or leave records for marketing.

6. Retention and deletion

Alarms, groups, leave allowances, manual sleep entries and saved recordings remain until you delete them or uninstall the app. Each Apple Health import uses the latest 180 days and replaces the local imported copy. Manual sleep entries are kept separately. Ring, stop and snooze history and schedule snapshots are kept for about 180 days. Finished timer data clears at the next check. Briefing dates and weather are held in memory for that session. The weather location and whether a briefing ran that day are stored locally. Choose the date-only option in briefing settings to remove the saved weather location.

You can delete alarms, groups, leave or sleep records and recordings in the app. When a recording is in use, the app first asks you to replace it for the affected alarms and confirms the resulting schedule. Deleting a group leaves its alarms. Losing Pro access does not automatically delete existing records or turn off existing alarms. Uninstalling removes local app data. Manage device backups through Apple. The developer cannot remotely read or delete those backups.

7. Your choices and updates to this policy

Manage alarm, camera, microphone, speech recognition and health permissions in system settings. Features that need those permissions may be unavailable if you turn them off. You can separately turn off morning briefings or leave expiry reminders, or stop playback. Built-in sounds and file import remain available without microphone permission.

The app is not directed at children and provides no account, social or advertising services for them. When features or data handling change, the developer updates this policy and provides notice where required.

Sunrise, sunset and location

You can select a city or explicitly request your current location once. The app requests location permission only while in use and does not request background access. If you decline, you can still select a city. Coordinates, place name and the selected time zone are stored locally to calculate sunrise and sunset offline and are not sent to the developer or calendar services or used for tracking. You can update or remove a saved location or revoke permission. Times are estimates and can vary with terrain and weather. Apple's system services handle location and time-zone matching. Matching a time zone uses the network and sends coordinates to Apple's geocoding service. Sunrise and sunset are then calculated on the device using the place's time zone.

Local leave notifications

You can choose several reminders 30, 14 or 7 days before leave expires and set a time. With notification permission, iOS displays the remaining days, leave type, expiry date and a suggestion on the device. The developer's servers do not push these notifications. You can turn them off or hide previews in system settings. Cancelling reminders, using up leave or deleting an allowance also removes its future notifications.

Free Pro experience and saved reports

The three-day Pro experience is claimed via a free, non-consumable App Store purchase and does not start a renewing subscription. Apple retains the claim transaction for your Apple Account. The app verifies the product, original transaction identifier and first claim time to determine when access ends. Reinstalling or changing devices with the same Apple Account does not restart the experience. The local Keychain and settings cache its timing and reminder state. A local record alone cannot unlock access. The app does not read your Apple Account password or send the claim to the developer's servers.

Opening a Pro weekly or monthly report saves a local snapshot with the alarm, sleep and schedule data needed for the report and comparisons. Snapshots persist after the experience ends and after older event history expires. You can delete snapshots individually. Removing sleep data or local Health copies also removes the related sleep entries from report snapshots and may change the totals.

Photo backgrounds for in-app ringing

The system photo picker gives the app access only to the photo you select, without granting access to the entire library. If you choose to take a photo, the app asks for camera permission. Captures are not saved automatically to Photos. If you decline camera permission, you can still pick a photo or use a built-in background. The app adjusts orientation and size, saves a local JPEG copy without original EXIF or location metadata, and does not upload it. You can remove an imported copy in the app. The original photo is unaffected. Apple handles any download from iCloud under its own policies.

Home Screen widget backgrounds

The system photo picker reads only the selected photo. You may also choose to use the camera, subject to permission. Photos taken for the widget are not automatically saved to Photos. The app saves a resized JPEG with standard orientation and no original EXIF or GPS metadata in the local directory shared by the app and widget. Small and medium widgets have separate backgrounds. Saving a replacement, removing it or restoring the default clears the corresponding old copy. The widget uses only the alarm display snapshot, holiday calendar and background settings it needs. Sleep and health records and recordings are not shared with it.
